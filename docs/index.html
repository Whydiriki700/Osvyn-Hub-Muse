Features

Built for people who run many accounts

More than a pile of tabs. Osvyn manages the sessions, memory, and identity of every Muse account you use each day.

01CORE ISOLATION

Profiles for Every Account

Add profiles up to your plan limit, up to unlimited on the Hub plan. Each profile is a separate, persistent Muse login session.

02MEMORY ENGINE

DeepSleep RAM Saving

A profile that stays inactive for 3 minutes goes to sleep and frees its RAM. One click wakes it back up.

03Ctrl+1..9

QuickSwitch

Move between profiles as fast as you change your mind Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9, no mouse, no reload wait.

04

Aura ID & Presence Dot

Every profile carries its own identity color and presence dot, so you always know which account is active, asleep, or open.

05

AutoResume

The app remembers your last profile and tab layout. Reopen it and everything is exactly where you left it.

06

Profile Vault

Back up and restore your profile list. Moving to a new computer no longer means rebuilding everything from zero.

07PRIVACY

Locked Until Activated

Osvyn stays fully locked until a valid MuseKey activates it on your machine. Every profile session stays separate, so accounts never mix.

08PLAN UPDATES

Updates in Your Window

New versions are released within your plan update window. You install them when it suits your work, with no surprise restarts.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about licensing, offline behavior, and system requirements.

Are Muse accounts included in the price?

No. Osvyn is a profile manager app. Each profile signs in with your own Muse account, on the official Muse page.

How do I activate Osvyn?

Buy a plan and your MuseKey arrives by email. Enter it once on the app start screen and the app unlocks to your plan right away.

Can I use Osvyn offline?

Yes. After activation the app works offline. It checks in occasionally when a connection is available, and a 7-day offline grace period keeps you working when the internet drops.

What is the Hub?

The Hub is the most complete Osvyn tier: one space where profiles and agents from all accounts gather and work together, with unlimited profiles. The Hub is a separate plan, ordered through WhatsApp.

Which computers are supported?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with the WebView2 Runtime and an internet connection for signing in and using Muse.
